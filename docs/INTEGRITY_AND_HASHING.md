# Integrity and hashing

The production canonical representation must define field order, Unicode normalization, whitespace treatment, numeric/currency normalization, milestone ordering, and version identifiers. Hash records must store algorithm, canonicalization version, agreement version, and timestamp.

Private document contents stay off-chain. A future optional Sepolia anchor may prove that a record existed at a point in time. Neither a hash nor a parity result establishes legal enforceability.
